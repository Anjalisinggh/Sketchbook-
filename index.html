    About this book

    Notes

    A pocket sketchbook carried everywhere — drawn on the spot, in whatever light there was.

    Each plate starts as a quick fineliner line, then gets loosened up with a few washes of watercolour while the paper is still damp. Nothing is redrawn; the wobbles stay in.

    Pick up the glass and look closely — the grain of the paper, where the pigment pooled, the lines that overshot their corners.

    Paper
    Cold-press, 300 gsm, landscape spread
    Ink
    Sepia fineliner, 0.1 – 0.5
    Paint
    Twelve-pan travel set, one water brush
